This offer gives Student Beans members a 20% discount for travel Monday - Thursday or a 10% discount for travel Friday - Sunday off a full priced National Express Standard or Fully Flexible fare.

The offer can only be used for a maximum of 4 passengers per booking Journeys to events and special services are all excluded from this promotion.

Why we love National Express

Head of Product Marketing

Honestly, National Express is a uni student's secret weapon. Coaches are comfy, the Wi-Fi means you can actually get work done on the way, and with your National Express student discount, the price difference compared to train tickets is genuinely hard to ignore. Book ahead and you can find some seriously good fares — ideal for squeezing in a long weekend without blowing your budget.

FAQs

How do I get my National Express student discount?

Verify through Student Beans, get your code, and apply it when booking on the National Express website or app. Your National Express student discount gets sorted before you pay.

What is National Express's refund and exchange policy?

Ticket flexibility depends on the fare type you book. Some tickets are fully refundable, others can be amended for a fee. Check the fare conditions at the time of booking for the full details.

Where does National Express travel to?

National Express covers over 500 destinations across the UK, including most major cities, towns, airports, and popular uni cities like Manchester, Leeds, Birmingham, and Bristol.

How much cheaper is National Express than the train?

Generally speaking, coaches are significantly cheaper than trains for the same route, especially for longer journeys. Booking early gets you the best fares — sometimes by quite a margin.

Does National Express have Wi-Fi on coaches?

Yes, most National Express coaches have free Wi-Fi on board, along with plug sockets. Great for catching up on lectures or getting ahead on that essay you've been putting off.

Can I take luggage on a National Express coach?

You can take one piece of hand luggage and one piece of hold luggage for free. Additional bags can usually be added for a small fee at the time of booking.
