FAQs

How do I use my Trainline student discount?

Grab your Trainline discount code, add it at checkout, and your student discount will apply if your ticket qualifies.

Can I use Trainline for coaches too?

Yep. Trainline covers coach travel as well as trains, so you can compare options and pick what suits your budget and timing.

Do I need a Railcard as well?

Not always. A Railcard is separate and can reduce eligible fares, but the Trainline student discount is applied using your discount code during booking.

How much is a student railcard?

A 16-25 Railcard costs £35 and saves you 1/3 on rail fares throughout Great Britain for a whole year.

Will Trainline show delays and platform changes?

Yes. Trainline gives live journey updates, including disruption alerts, so you’re not guessing at the station like it’s a suspense film.

Can I change or refund my Trainline ticket?

Depends on the ticket type and operator rules. Trainline will show the conditions before you pay, and you can manage changes in the app where available.

Is the Trainline discount code valid on all routes?

Some tickets, routes, or operators may be excluded. If it’s eligible, Trainline will apply your student discount when you enter the discount code at checkout.

Is Trainline only for UK travel?

Nope! You can also use Trainline to book international travel and coach trips across Europe.

Do I need to print my Trainline tickets?

Usually not. Most journeys offer mobile or eTickets you can store in the app, so your phone is your ticket. Just make sure it's charged before you hit the barriers.
